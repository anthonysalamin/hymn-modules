# Hymn Design

> Hymn is an independent Swiss branding and design agency based in Lausanne and Zürich. We specialize in creating impactful, lasting visual identities — transforming brands through strategy, design, and instinct.

Hymn Design SA was founded in Lausanne, Switzerland. We work with Swiss and international clients across branding, visual identity, digital experiences, motion design, signage, packaging, and brand communication. Our approach is rooted in intuition, deep exploration of a brand's DNA, and honest creative partnership.

## Services

- **Brand Strategy**: Define and solidify the DNA of your brand or project
- **Brand Identity**: Reveal the emotional identity and create a unique visual territory
- **Visual Design**: Create the elements that define visual identity (logo, typography, color systems)
- **Brand Guidelines**: Establish comprehensive guidelines for consistent brand expression
- **Digital Experiences**: Website design, UI/UX, and digital touchpoints
- **Motion Design**: Animation and video content for broadcast, social media, and web
- **Physical Design**: In-store design, environmental graphics, and spatial branding
- **Signage**: Wayfinding and environmental signage systems
- **Packaging**: Product packaging design
- **Naming**: Brand and product naming
